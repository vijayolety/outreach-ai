Screen 2 of 10 · Lead upload + validation 
Upload leads — errors fixed before AI touches anything 
Dedicated validation screen with inline error table. Supported fields checklist anchors the user on what

Screen 3 of 10 · Campaign setup 
Sender, tone, CTA, follow-ups — keep it tight 
Tone and CTA type are the two controls that most change output quality with minimal friction. Business 
type and location are auto-detected from the lead list and editable. Nothing else needed. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 


---PAGE---

Screen 4 of 10 · Email review + trust layer ← most critical screen 
Left sidebar to browse all leads · Main panel to review + edit · "Why this email?" on every 
draft 
The sidebar shows all leads with a per-lead status dot (green = ready, amber = needs review, red = 
failed). Clicking any lead loads their email in the main workspace. Founders approve all at once; 
operators review one-by-one.

Screen 5 of 10 · Pre-send confirmation 
Final gate before emails go out — no surprises 
Summary of what's about to happen. Rate limit, schedule, sender identity, follow-up sequence all visible. 
The kill switch is also present here. One clear launch button.

Screen 7 of 10 · Campaign monitor 
30-second daily check-in — hot leads first, funnel second 
Designed to answer "did anything happen?" in one glance. Notification banner surfaces hot leads. Stats

Screen 8 of 10 · All leads table 
Full lead list — filterable by status, with follow-up due date column 
Filter tabs (All / Hot / Warm / Cold) let operators focus. Follow-up due date column tells them what's 
imminent. "Manual ping" on warm leads gives operators a direct action alongside the automated 
sequence. Export always available.

Screen 9 of 10 · Hot lead detail 
Reply thread + AI-drafted response + activity timeline + next action 
Full conversation thread on the left. AI-drafted reply pre-loaded and editable on the right — "Why this 
draft?" explainability included. Activity timeline shows the full lead journey. Attach booking link in one click. 
Status labels let the operator triage and close fast.

Screen 10 of 10 · Settings 
Gmail OAuth, send throttle, follow-up toggles, session security 
Gemini's addition — this deserves its own screen. The max emails/hour slider is a spam-risk control. 
Follow-up toggles let users disable sequences globally. Session-only auth note addresses the "no 
credential storage" security requirement from the BRD.
